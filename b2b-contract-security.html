Here’s how it fits your operation

Built for the three sides
of a guarding business.

Your training program, your clients, and your guards. Defencify is built to serve all three.

For your training program

Defencify handles your foundational training and continuing education, including state-approved courses, certification tracking, and the regulatory training your compliance officer documents every cycle. That frees your in-person trainers for the live, hands-on work: defensive tactics, scenario-based drills, and skill training.

For your clients

Site-specific post orders, productized into interactive training every guard completes before they’re scheduled. The Operator Console gives your account managers the reporting and exports they need when a client asks who’s trained, on what, and when.

For your guards

Defencify runs on any device a guard already carries: phone, tablet, or computer. Training happens between shifts, on breaks, or at home, so more guards complete what’s assigned. Engagement-verified mechanics confirm the learning actually happened.

Here’s the operational difference

Where contract guarding
gets measured.

Operators running Defencify across contract guarding see the program show up in how guards perform on post, in how often clients renew, and in how long guards stay in the role.

Higher performance on post

Defencify training is built for retention, not just completion. Guards retain the material, apply it in the moment, and bring the situational awareness and execution clients notice every shift.

Stronger client retention

When guards perform well, clients notice. They renew with operators whose guards are present, prepared, and professional, and they recommend you when other facilities ask.

Better guard retention

Guards who feel prepared on day one stay longer. Lower turnover means lower hiring costs, a more experienced workforce, and a stronger answer in every renewal conversation.
