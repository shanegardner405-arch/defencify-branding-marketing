Common questions

What operators ask
before they start.

How is pricing calculated?

Per-guard, per-year pricing based on your active officer headcount. Annual contracts billed annually or semi-annually so your training budget is predictable from day one.

Can I upgrade after starting on Foundations?

Yes. All plans are designed to layer. Add PostReady, ProficiencyCheck, or Academy Architect as your operation grows. You keep your data and your guard records across every upgrade.

What makes Defencify different?

Defencify is purpose-built for private security, not adapted from another vertical, with a plan for every operation from single-site to multi-state.
