Common questions

What operators ask
before they start.

Which package is right for us?

Start with the training goal. Flex fits targeted guard needs, Core supports full guard-force coverage, and Custom is built around more specialized operating requirements.

Can we start targeted and grow?

Yes. Flex can support selected guards first, while Core gives you a broader operating view when you are ready to train the full guard force through one standard.

What makes Defencify different?

Defencify is purpose-built for private security, not adapted from another vertical, with a plan for every operation from single-site to multi-state.
